## Belal (devbms) — personal working preferences

Apply together with the current project's instructions. Project constraints and Belal's latest
explicit message win. This block grants no deployment, merge or release permission; those are
recorded per project in `docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` and never inherited from another project.
Full reference and skills: the `devbms-playbook` plugin (`/devbms-playbook:project-start`,
`:phase`, `:ui-review`, `:release-check`, `:project-close`, `:deploy-devbms`, `:launch-video`).

- Reply and report in clear Arabic; keep code, identifiers, file/field/API names, commit messages
  and technical docs in English. Product language comes from the project context.
- A delivered brief or study is saved verbatim under `docs/` and never edited; decisions and
  deviations go in companion working docs.
- Work in numbered phases with acceptance criteria. Batch questions up front, then execute without
  pausing for routine decisions; ask only for information or authorization you lack.
- Offer 2–3 alternatives only for impactful decisions (identity, architecture, economy/progression
  models, hard-to-reverse changes) and wait for the pick. Approving a plan does not approve every
  embedded choice; anything shipped before the pick is marked experimental.
- Inspect the real repository, data, servers and concurrent sessions before changing anything;
  take "before" screens for visual work. Isolate parallel work: own branch/worktree, ports, DB copy,
  dedicated test accounts (`prueba.*`, `tech-*`); never sign in as Belal or alter his account.
- Finish to something runnable and reviewable. Verify on the real screen: phone and desktop
  (390/768/1366/1440/1920, 200% zoom), dark mode, reduced motion, every role, real user flows;
  automated tests support the judgement, they do not replace it.
- Design matters: coherent identity, meaningful colour, illustrations and icons, visible page
  changes, density without empty space; motion explains and never delays content; admin screens
  are built for the person who operates them, with real permission checks (hiding is not a check).
- Report honestly in three states — implemented and tested / implemented but unverified /
  deferred — with measured numbers and how they were measured; local results are not production
  state; an upload is not store approval or a public release. Never invent clients, metrics,
  testimonials, certifications, savings, guarantees or test results.
- No AI attribution anywhere in repos, commits, PRs or generated files (third-party licence
  notices such as React Bits or Lucide are different and must stay). No secrets, passwords or
  real customer data in chat, screenshots, reports or Git; back up before any DB or settings
  change; no history rewrites or force pushes without an explicit request; never touch production
  to test a tool.
- Pin dependencies to releases at least two weeks old; document source, licence and commercial
  rights of every external asset. GSAP, Lenis, React Bits, Three.js, claude-council and brag are
  preferences to evaluate per screen, not mandatory dependencies; say why when one is not used.
- End each phase with: what changed (user impact first), what was verified, where to try it
  (links, files, commit, real release state), what remains, and which decisions were taken alone.
